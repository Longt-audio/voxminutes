## 2. The service

VoxMinutes is a **local-first** meeting-notes tool offering two categories of features.

### 2.1 Local features — free and offline

Microphone and system-audio recording; local speech recognition, translation and summarization;
history management, re-transcription, merging, export and PDF printing.

Local features **consume no credits** and require no network connection (except to download models
the first time). Models run on your own computer.

### 2.2 Remote features — consume credits

Remote speech recognition (streaming and batch), remote translation, remote summarization and
speech synthesis. These require a license key and the "Enable remote service" switch.

Remote features depend on third-party AI providers. Their availability, latency and accuracy are
outside our control and we cannot guarantee them.

### 2.3 Your own "Custom API"

You may configure your own OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic-compatible endpoint. In that case traffic
does not pass through our servers, **no credits are consumed**, and availability, cost and data
handling are a matter between you and that provider.
