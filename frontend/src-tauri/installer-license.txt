VoxMinutes 用户协议与隐私政策（摘要）

安装或使用本软件前，请阅读以下要点。完整的《用户协议》与《隐私政策》见文末链接，
继续安装即表示你已阅读并同意这两份文件。

一、本软件会把哪些数据传出你的电脑
  · 使用「本地模型」时，录音与文本全程不离开你的电脑。
  · 只有当你主动选择「远程」模型或开启「远程服务」时：
      - 语音识别：音频片段（流式）或整段录音（离线重识别）会上传到我们的服务器，
        并由服务器转发给上游 AI 服务商；用于临时托管的音频最长 2 小时后自动删除。
      - 翻译 / 会议总结：待处理的文本会上传并转发给上游 AI 服务商。
  · 上游服务商位于中国大陆（阿里云百炼、火山引擎、小米 MiMo 等）与美国（Deepgram），
    我们的服务器位于中国香港 —— 因此属于个人信息跨境传输。不同意请只使用本地模型。
  · 我们不会将你的音频或文本用于训练模型，也不会用于本摘要未说明的用途。

二、我们不收集什么
  不需要注册账号；不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、剪贴板；
  不读取你电脑上的其他文件；软件内未集成任何第三方统计分析 SDK。

三、你的录音存在哪里
  录音与转写保存在你自己选择的录音目录，本地数据库与日志保存在应用数据目录。
  【注意】卸载本软件不会自动删除这些文件，如需删除请手动清理。

VoxMinutes Terms of Service & Privacy Policy (Summary)

Please read the following before installing. The full documents are linked below;
by continuing the installation you confirm that you have read and agree to them.

1. What leaves your computer
   · With "local" models, your audio and text never leave your computer.
   · Only when you explicitly select a "remote" model / enable the remote service:
       - Speech recognition: audio segments (streaming) or the whole recording (batch)
         are uploaded to our server and forwarded to an upstream AI provider.
         Temporarily hosted audio is deleted automatically within 2 hours.
       - Translation / summarization: the text to be processed is uploaded and forwarded.
   · Upstream providers are located in mainland China (Alibaba Bailian, Volcengine,
     Xiaomi MiMo, ...) and the United States (Deepgram); our server is in Hong Kong.
     This is a cross-border transfer. If you do not agree, use local models only.
   · We never use your audio or text to train models, nor for any purpose not stated here.

2. What we do NOT collect
   No account registration; no name, phone, email, location, contacts or clipboard;
   no access to other files on your computer; no third-party analytics SDK.

3. Where your recordings are stored
   Recordings and transcripts live in the folder you choose; the local database and logs
   live in the application data directory. NOTE: uninstalling does NOT delete them.
