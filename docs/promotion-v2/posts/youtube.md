## Description

VoxMinutes is a 100% free, open-source, local-first meeting assistant for Windows. It records system audio and microphone together, transcribes in real time, translates into 13 languages, and generates AI meeting summaries — all offline on your device.

🔗 Links:
GitHub: https://github.com/Longt-audio/voxminutes
Download: https://github.com/Longt-audio/voxminutes/releases

🛠️ Tech stack:
Tauri 2, Rust, Next.js, sherpa-onnx, llama.cpp, SQLite

💡 Why I built it:
Most meeting tools either charge per minute, upload your audio to the cloud, or can only record one audio source. VoxMinutes solves all three.
